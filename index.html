Where the numbers come from

Every number says where it's from

Each card carries a badge, so you always know if a value came from the manual, from the bench or from a kit maker.

FSM ✓

Transcribed from the Nissan factory service manual page cited on the card. 2003 350Z, revised April 2004.

BENCH SPEC

Tested on the bench and labelled that way. Never mixed in with the manual's numbers.

ARP KIT · JWT SPEC

From the kit maker's own instructions, for those parts only, in the manual's order.

Torque applies when tightening. Loosening has no torque spec, only the order. Always check a spec matches your model year.

FAQ

Questions

Which cars does it cover?

The first build is the Nissan 350Z (Z33). Its specs come from the 2003 factory service manual with the VQ35DE engine, so check each one against your model year. The 2007–09 cars have the VQ35HR engine, which is different on top; their build is coming soon.

Will you add my car?

That's the plan. Torque HQ is built to hold more builds. Tell us what you drive when you join the list; it helps decide which build comes next.

Do I need an app store?

No. It runs in Safari or Chrome. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Once it's on your phone, the whole manual is stored on it, drawings included.

How do beta invites work?

Join the list and we'll look at your build on Instagram. When the open beta starts, you'll get an email with your invite link.

Is this made by Nissan?

No. Torque HQ is an independent reference and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Nissan or any other carmaker.
